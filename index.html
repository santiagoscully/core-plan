Endurance work, about 10 minutes. Go straight from one exercise to the next and rest 90 sec between rounds. End the set if your low back lifts off the floor. Never do this before a strength session.

Benchmarks

Rules

  • Train core after your main lifts or on separate days, so heavy squats and deadlifts don't suffer.
  • Move up only when every set is clean: ribs down, no low-back arching, steady breathing.
  • Carries are measured in distance, not time: 130 ft is about 50 steps, under a minute of walking.
  • Rest 60–90 sec between sets, 2 min on dragon flags and front lever work.
  • Deload for a week, cutting volume about 40%, if your low back feels beat up.
